quiz Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Does ShreePdf Viewer collect any of my PDF data?

No. ShreePdf Viewer is 100% offline and private. It collects zero analytics, zero telemetry, and never uploads your files to any server.

Q: What Windows versions are supported?

ShreePdf Viewer supports Windows 10 and Windows 11 on both 64-bit (x64) and ARM64 devices.

Q: How do I open PDF files in ShreePdf Viewer?

You can drag and drop any PDF file directly into the ShreePdf Viewer window, or click the "📂 Open PDF" button in the top toolbar.

Q: How do I request new features or vector rendering improvements?

You can submit feature requests on our GitHub repository.
