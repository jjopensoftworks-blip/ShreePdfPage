Privacy Policy

ShreePdf Viewer · Effective Date: August 15, 2026

ShreePdf Viewer is engineered to render PDF documents with 100% privacy and sub-10ms vector speed. It collects nothing, transmits nothing, and executes entirely on your own local device.

What we collect

Nothing. ShreePdf Viewer does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, PDF file contents, page texts, annotations, search history, IP addresses, or device analytics. There are no user accounts, no tracking cookies, no telemetry, and no advertising trackers.

Network access

ShreePdf Viewer operates completely offline. The application makes zero network requests during document viewing, parsing, and vector rendering. Your files never touch any external server or cloud service.

Your files

All rendering calculations and vector layout math occur locally in memory on your hardware via SkiaSharp graphics primitives. Your documents are opened in read-only mode and stay entirely on your machine.

Data storage

ShreePdf Viewer does not maintain cloud databases or remote profiles. User preferences (such as Dark/Light mode state or zoom defaults) are saved locally on your device in standard application settings.

Microsoft Store & Platform Distribution

If you download ShreePdf Viewer through the Microsoft Store, Microsoft handles installation metrics pursuant to the Microsoft Privacy Statement. ShreePdf Viewer itself collects no personal identity metrics.

Children's privacy

The app collects no data from any user, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this privacy policy is revised, updates will be posted to this exact page URL with a revised "Effective Date".

Contact & Support

For privacy questions or support inquiries, contact jjopensoftworks@gmail.com or visit our Support Center.
